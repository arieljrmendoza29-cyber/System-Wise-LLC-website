How our fees work

Your tax preparation fee depends on how complex your return is, and your preparer will quote it before filing. Nothing is filed until you know your fee.

If you choose to pay from your refund, a one-time $278 software, e-file and banking fee applies for the current tax year and is deducted from your refund.

IRS forms are provided by the Internal Revenue Service and open on irs.gov. If you have questions about which form applies to you, contact your preparer before filing.
