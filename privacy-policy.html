System Wise LLC (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal and business information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard information when you visit our website at https://systemwisellc.com (the “Site”) or engage our IT infrastructure, system integration, cloud, cybersecurity, and managed technology consulting services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to communicate with you, evaluate your technical needs, and deliver our services effectively:

  • Information You Provide Directly: When you submit a consultation request, fill out our contact form, or communicate with us via email or phone, we may collect your full name, company name, work email address, phone number, service interests, and project details.
  • Technical & Usage Data: When you browse our Site, standard server logs and analytics tools may automatically collect non-identifying technical data, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, and timestamps.
  • Client Onboarding & Service Records: When you engage System Wise LLC for IT support, network configuration, cloud migration, or managed services, we maintain necessary technical documentation, system architecture notes, and authorized administrative contact records strictly for service delivery.

2. How We Use Your Information

System Wise LLC uses the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries, schedule consultations, and provide project estimates.
  • Deliver, configure, maintain, and support requested IT and business technology services.
  • Improve the performance, security, and usability of systemwisellc.com.
  • Send administrative updates, service notifications, or invoicing records related to active engagements.
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and security obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business contact information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies & Analytics

Our Site may use lightweight, essential cookies or privacy-conscious analytics tools to understand general site traffic and ensure fast load times across devices. You can disable or clear cookies at any time through your browser settings without restricting access to core Site information.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We share information only when strictly required to operate our website or fulfill your service requests:

  • Infrastructure & Hosting Partners: Website hosting, domain DNS management, and secure email providers that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality standards.
  • Authorized Technology Vendors: Cloud platforms (such as Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace), software vendors, or hardware suppliers when you explicitly authorize us to provision or manage accounts on your behalf.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, subpoena, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of System Wise LLC, our clients, or others.

5. Data Security & Retention

Established in 2021, System Wise LLC applies industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards—including encrypted HTTPS transmission, access controls, and multi-factor authentication—to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

We retain inquiry and client records only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Policy, maintain active service support, or satisfy legal and accounting requirements.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or outdated contact details.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to active contractual or legal retention obligations.
  • Opt out of non-essential follow-up communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us directly using the details below.

7. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to external vendor portals, documentation, or partner websites. System Wise LLC is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party sites, and we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, technical infrastructure, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
